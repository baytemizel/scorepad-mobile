# Privacy Policy — ScorePad

_Last updated: 2026_

ScorePad ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This document explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Anonymous use (Guest mode)

You can use ScorePad without creating an account. In guest mode, **all match data is stored locally on your device only** and is never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes that data.

## 2. Account data

If you sign in with Apple, Google, or Facebook (earlier versions also offered email and password), we receive your email address and a unique account identifier from the provider. We **do not** receive your password. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only receive an anonymous relay address. Facebook also shares your public profile name and picture; the app does not display them.

Facebook Login is provided by Meta. Meta's privacy policy applies when you use it: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

We use Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to manage your account. Their privacy policy applies in addition to ours: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## 3. Cloud sync (Premium only)

If you purchase Premium, completed match records are synced to **Firebase Firestore** so they are available across your devices. Synced data includes:

- Team names and scores
- Sport, period information, and event log
- Match start/finish timestamps

We do **not** collect: contacts, photos, location, microphone, or any data outside of what you explicitly enter into the app.

## 4. Analytics

We use **Firebase Analytics** to understand which features are used (e.g. how often each sport is selected). Events are sent without any personally identifying information beyond an anonymous device-level identifier. You can opt out by signing out and using the app in guest mode.

## 5. Advertising

Free users see banner and interstitial advertisements served by **Google AdMob**.

- **iOS**: We ask permission via App Tracking Transparency before any *personalized* advertising. Declining only results in less-relevant (non-personalized) ads — the ads themselves remain.
- **Android**: We respect the system-level "Opt out of Ads Personalization" toggle.

We never see your ad-click history. Google's AdMob privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. In-app purchase

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store** depending on your platform. We do **not** receive your payment details. We only receive a confirmation receipt that the purchase succeeded.

## 7. Children

ScorePad is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete the data immediately.

## 8. Data retention & removal

- **Local data**: removed automatically when you uninstall the app.
- **Cloud data**: retained while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time in the app under **Settings → Delete account**; the deletion is immediate. If you can't access the app, email **support@scorepad.best** and we will delete within 30 days.
- **Analytics**: aggregated, retained per Firebase Analytics defaults (14 months).

## 9. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / similar)

Depending on your jurisdiction you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
- Export your data
- Object to processing

Email **support@scorepad.best** to exercise any of these rights.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be highlighted in-app the next time you open it. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
