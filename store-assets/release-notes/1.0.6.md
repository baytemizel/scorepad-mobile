# ScorePad 1.0.6 — What's New

Store copy for the Play Console and App Store Connect "What's New" fields.
Kept under 500 characters so the same text fits both (Play's limit).

## English

• Sign in with Facebook.
• Already have an account with the same email? Signing in another way now opens that same account, with your match history and Premium.
• Simpler sign-in: pick a provider or keep using the app as a guest.

## Türkçe

• Facebook ile giriş eklendi.
• Aynı e-postayla zaten bir hesabınız varsa, başka bir yöntemle giriş yaptığınızda artık aynı hesap açılıyor; maç geçmişiniz ve Premium'unuz korunuyor.
• Giriş sadeleşti: bir giriş yöntemi seçin ya da misafir olarak devam edin.

---

Both texts work on either store as written.

Email/password sign-in is switched off in this release, which the copy leaves
out. Anyone who signed up with an email and password and later signs out can't
sign back in with it. Point them to support@scorepad.best if they ask.

The app ships 15 locales; the other 13 can be translated from the English text
above if the stores are given per-locale release notes.
